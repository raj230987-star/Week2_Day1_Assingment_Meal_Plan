3. Daily healthy habits
A. Water intake
- Aim for approximately 2–2.5 litres of fluids daily, unless medically advised otherwise.
- Drink a glass of water after waking up.
- Drink water regularly throughout the day.
- Have buttermilk without added salt or sugar when appropriate.
- Increase fluids during hot weather and exercise.
B. Physical activity










Walking

30–40 minutes of brisk walking on most days.













Strength exercises

2–3 days per week: chair squats, wall push-ups and light resistance exercises.













Stretching

5–10 minutes of gentle stretching daily.



C. Sleep recommendations
- Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep every night.
- Maintain a consistent sleeping schedule.
- Avoid heavy meals immediately before bedtime.
- Reduce mobile phone use before sleeping.
D. Healthy eating habits
1. Eat breakfast regularly.
2. Include a protein source in every main meal.
3. Fill approximately half your lunch and dinner plate with vegetables.
4. Prefer whole fruits instead of fruit juices.
5. Reduce added sugar in coffee and tea.
6. Avoid frequent fried snacks and bakery foods.
7. Eat slowly and avoid overeating.

C. Meal preparation tips
1. Soak green gram and chickpeas overnight for easier cooking.
2. Prepare dosa and idli batter in advance.
3. Wash and chop vegetables for 2–3 days at a time.
4. Boil eggs in advance and refrigerate safely.
5. Prepare sambar and kootu using different vegetables.
6. Measure rice portions instead of serving directly from the cooking vessel.
7. Avoid adding excess oil during tempering.
D. Important health considerations
At age 55, preserving muscle mass is especially important while losing weight.
- Include adequate protein in all main meals.
- Do not skip meals or follow crash diets.
- Combine walking with strength exercises.
- Monitor body weight once a week under similar conditions.
- If you have diabetes, kidney disease, high blood pressure or other medical conditions, get individual dietary advice from a registered dietitian or doctor.

7. Final recommendation
For your current weight of 70 kg and height of 5 feet 5 inches, focus on gradual weight reduction rather than rapid weight loss.

Your main priorities should be:
1. Controlled rice and carbohydrate portions.
2. Protein-rich foods such as dal, green gram, chickpeas, paneer, curd and eggs.
3. Daily vegetables and fruits.
4. Regular walking and strength exercises.
5. Consistent sleep and hydration.
One important point: This is a general nutrition plan, not a clinically calculated prescription. Your exact calorie and protein requirements depend on health history, exercise intensity and other individual factors. A dietitian can fine-tune the portions.

The objective is to build a healthy eating routine that you can comfortably maintain for months, not just seven days.
