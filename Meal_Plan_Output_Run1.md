3. Simple meal preparation tips
1. Weekend preparation: Soak and cook chickpeas, green gram and black chana. Refrigerate in separate portions.
2. Breakfast preparation: Prepare idli/dosa batter in advance and refrigerate.
3. Vegetables: Wash, clean and refrigerate vegetables in separate containers.
4. Lunch preparation: Cook rice and prepare sambar or kootu in the morning. Carry curd separately.
5. Dinner preparation: Use pre-prepared batter, cooked pulses and chopped vegetables to finish dinner within 30 minutes.
6. Sundal preparation: Boil legumes in batches and use different varieties throughout the week.
4. Healthy cooking suggestions
- Prefer steaming, boiling and shallow cooking instead of deep frying.
- Keep oil moderate, approximately 3–4 teaspoons daily per person as a starting cooking allowance.
- Include protein sources such as dal, green gram, chana, karamani and paneer.
- Include vegetables in both lunch and dinner.
- Prefer whole fruits instead of fruit juices.
- Reduce added sugar in tea and coffee.
- Avoid excessive salt, pickle and packaged snacks.
- Drink water regularly, particularly in Chennai's hot weather.
- Combine healthy eating with regular walking and suitable strength exercises.
