## 3. Simple meal preparation tips

1. Weekend preparation: Clean and chop vegetables for the first few days and refrigerate them properly.
2. Prepare batter: Keep idli/dosa batter ready to save weekday cooking time.
3. Protein preparation: Soak green gram and black chana overnight when required.
4. Chicken and fish: Buy fresh, divide into meal-sized portions and refrigerate or freeze safely.
5. Lunch preparation: Cook rice and prepare one vegetable side dish along with sambar or kuzhambu.
6. Evening snacks: Keep seasonal fruits, boiled chana and sundal ready.
7. Dinner: Prefer simple preparations such as dosa, chapati, adai and vegetable dishes.

## 4. Healthy cooking suggestions

- Prefer steaming, boiling, grilling and light sautéing.
- Limit deep-fried foods and excess oil.
- Include vegetables in both lunch and dinner.
- Choose whole grains and millets regularly.
- Include protein sources such as eggs, chicken, fish, dal and legumes.
- Drink approximately 2–2.5 litres of water daily, adjusting for weather, activity and individual needs.
- Reduce excess sugar, sweets, bakery foods and sugary drinks.
- Include regular physical activity, such as walking, cycling or strength exercises.
- Maintain consistent meal timings and get adequate sleep.
